About me

The short version.

  • 28 years old
  • Canada

I'm Kevin, better known online as TehZombiJesus. I'm the person who ends up running the servers: setting them up, keeping them online, and figuring out why they stopped.

These days that means being CTO and systems administrator at Nox Interactive, managing the Ruenix Minecraft network, and a homelab at home that's slowly turning into a small datacentre. I'm not a developer. My side of things is infrastructure, coordination, and keeping communities organized.

When it's all running, I'm usually in a game, strictly for fun.
