Setup

The battlestation

A 57" super ultrawide, three screens above it, and a PC hanging under a standing desk. Every part chosen to last.

The layout

Everything is mounted to the desk, so the whole setup rises and lowers together when I switch between sitting and standing.

  • Samsung Odyssey Neo G9 57"Dual 4K super ultrawide, mini-LED, 240 Hz, 1000R curve. Mounted on the MAGNUS Heavy Duty arm.
  • Three 22" screens aboveThin bezels, 1080p. The outer two turn in about 31° to follow the curve below.
  • Two Vertical Monitor StandsAbout 20 cm either side of centre, tall enough to clear the 57".
  • PC under the deskOn the Secretlab Premium PC Mount, so the floor stays clear.
  • One special cableA VESA-certified DP80LL DisplayPort cable, the only kind that runs the 57" at full 240 Hz.

The next build

Every part chosen, and why it made the cut. No RGB anywhere.

Core

CPU
Ryzen 7 9850X3D3D V-Cache is the best thing you can give a CPU for games. Top frame rates without running hot.
GPU
RTX 5090 Founders EditionThe 57" is basically two 4K screens side by side. It takes the strongest card made to drive it properly.
Board
Gigabyte X870E AORUS MasterBuilt for the X3D chips, with PCIe 5.0, Wi-Fi 7 and plenty of fast ports. Nothing to outgrow.
RAM
64 GB DDR5-6000, G.Skill6000 is the sweet spot for Ryzen. 64 GB so big games never run short, even with Discord and a browser open.

Storage

Windows
2 TB WD Black, PCIe 5.0Games are huge now. The fastest drive goes where the games live.
Linux
2 TB WD BlackLinux gets a drive of its own, with room for tools and projects.
Setup
Windows 11 and Ubuntu 26.04, each on its own driveWindows for gaming, Linux for tinkering. Separate drives mean an update on one can never break the other.

Power and cooling

PSU
Seasonic 1600 W, Noctua EditionThe 5090 can spike hard. Lots of headroom keeps it cool, quiet and stable, and Titanium efficiency wastes less power.
Cooler
Arctic Liquid Freezer III ProOne of the best-performing coolers out there, quiet, and no RGB.
Case
Fractal North XL MeshLots of airflow for a 5090, room for a clean build, and a wood front instead of lights.
UPS
CyberPower 1500 VA battery backupA power flicker shouldn't end a game or corrupt a drive. It gives time to save and shut down safely.

Rules of the build

The principles every part had to pass.

No RGB

Not dimmed, not turned off in software. Parts without it, from the start.

Quality over price

Built to last for years, not to hit the cheapest total.

Checked first

Every part verified for compatibility before anything is ordered.

Nothing recycled

Every part in the new build is new.

No last-minute swaps

Once a part is decided, it stays decided.

Moves with the desk

Screens, PC and cables all rise and lower together.
