The Crypt SMP gets a home in the Crypt

The Minecraft server now has its own section in the same Discord, with news, how to join, chat, and support tickets for in-game problems.

Crypt Keeper v1.1.0

The Crypt SMP is my own Minecraft server, and I didn't want a second Discord just for it. So it now lives inside the Crypt, in its own ⛏️ The Crypt SMP section. Crypt Keeper v1.1.0 builds and runs it.

Who sees what

  • News and how to join are open to everyone, read-only, so anybody can see what the server is about and how to connect.
  • Chat, suggestions, screenshots and a voice channel are for members with the new Crypt SMP role. Pick it in onboarding or under Channels & Roles.

Everything in the section is bilingual, English first and French underneath, like the rest of the Crypt.

Support tickets, for Minecraft only

The support channel has four buttons: help, bug report, report a player and ban appeal. Press one, fill in your Minecraft name and what happened, and Crypt Keeper opens a private ticket that only you and the moderators can see. Press Close when it's sorted. You can have one open ticket at a time.

Tickets exist only for the Minecraft server. Anything else in the Crypt still goes to a moderator by direct message, and the older posts that mentioned tickets were cleaned up.

The server address

The how-to-join post shows the Java and Bedrock addresses and the version, and I update it with a single command whenever something changes.

Join the Crypt to see it.
