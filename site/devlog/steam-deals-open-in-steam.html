Devlog · October 12, 2026

Game deals: Steam only, with an Open in Steam button

Crypt Keeper's game deals now post only Steam games, and every post has a button that opens the game's Steam page.

Crypt Keeper v1.4.1 · Crypt Keeper v1.4.2

Crypt Keeper posts the best PC game deals in the Crypt. Two small changes in v1.4.1 and v1.4.2 make those posts easier to use.

An Open in Steam button

Every deal post now has an Open in Steam button under it. One tap and you're on the game's store page, ready to buy. The same button is on the DM you get when a game on your /wishlist drops to your price.

Steam only, for now

Deals used to come from several stores, and not every one could link straight to the game. For now Crypt Keeper only posts Steam deals, and only for games that have a Steam ID, so every post opens the real Steam page. Epic, GOG, Humble and the other stores are paused. I can switch them back on later with a single setting.

What did not change

  • The filters are the same: a minimum discount, a maximum price and a minimum Steam rating, set by staff with /deals filters.
  • /deal, /wishlist, the deal ping roles and the big sales calendar all work as before.

Want a ping for deals? Press the 🔔 button under any deal, or use /deal-pings. Join the Crypt to see it.
